# Business Models for Jarvis AI Assistant

## Overview
This document outlines the various business models that can be used to monetize Jarvis AI Assistant deployments.

## Service Offerings

### 1. Deployment Services
- **Setup Fee**: One-time fee for initial deployment
- **Customization**: Additional fee for custom features
- **Training**: Onboarding and training services

### 2. Support & Maintenance
- **Basic Support**: Email support, documentation
- **Premium Support**: Priority response, dedicated support
- **Managed Service**: Fully managed hosting and maintenance

### 3. Feature Tiers
- **Starter**: Basic features, limited support
- **Professional**: Advanced features, priority support
- **Enterprise**: Custom features, dedicated infrastructure

## Revenue Streams

### Recurring Revenue
- Monthly/annual subscription fees
- Support contract renewals
- Feature upgrade fees

### One-Time Fees
- Initial setup and deployment
- Custom development
- Training and onboarding

## Pricing Strategy

### Freemium Model
- Basic deployment: Free
- Premium features: Paid
- Enterprise features: Higher tier

### Value-Based Pricing
- Price based on value delivered to client
- Performance-based pricing models
- ROI-focused pricing
